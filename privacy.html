Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026

In short: what you log stays on your phone. Backups go to your own Google Drive or a file you keep. If you choose to sign in, only your name and email are saved. There are no ads and nothing is sold.

What stays on your phone

Your vehicles, fill-ups, expenses, services, income, odometer readings, reminders, trips, parts, documents, lists, photos of receipts and documents, and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read them. We have no server that receives them.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them, so keep a backup.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Location: to pick the pump you're at and to put trip stops on a map. Only while you use those features.
  • Notifications: for reminders, summaries and update alerts.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): optional, to notice when your phone connects to your car, for trip and reading prompts.
  • Install apps: so the app can install its own updates when you tap Update.

Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in. Everything works the same.
  • Sign out any time under More. To have your profile deleted, send a request through More → Report a problem.
  • Turn off Drive backup, or delete the backup from your Google Drive settings (Manage apps).
  • Location and notification permissions can be turned off in Android settings.

Children

The app is meant for vehicle owners and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and mentioned in the app's What's new.

Contact
