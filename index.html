Made for how you actually fill up

Built around Indian pumps, rupees and roads, for one rider or a family with a car and a bike.

Fill-ups in seconds

Type the amount; the price and litres follow. The nearest pump and the way you usually pay are picked for you, and a scanned receipt fills the form. A fill-up that looks wrong is caught before it's saved.

Real mileage

Worked out full tank to full tank, in km/L or L/100 km, with your next fill-up predicted. A tank well below your usual gets a heads-up.

What's due, together

Reminders by kilometres or date, and parts by wear: tyres, battery, filters, brake fluid. Things due around the same time show as one visit, logged in one go.

Documents, always on you

Insurance, PUC, RC and licence with their PDFs, even offline. Add the insurer's PDF and the policy details fill in. Renewals bought early are kept in order, behind a fingerprint lock if you like.

Budgets that look ahead

A monthly budget, limits per category (fuel, service, insurance by the year), and a forecast: at this pace, about ₹6,200 by month end.

Reports worth reading

Spending, mileage and fuel price trends, which pump is cheaper for the same fuel, this month against the last, and a summary picture to share.

Trips

Distance, cost, mileage and stops for each ride, the route recorded if you want it, the cost split with friends, and one tap to ride the same route again.

FASTag

Balance, tolls and the annual pass from your bank's SMS, in a passbook by day. A missed call to your bank checks the balance.

Tidy records

Find entries with no pump or payment and fill them in together. Check my data spots typos, missed fill-ups and entries saved twice.

Your logbook stays yours

Everything you log is stored on your phone. There's no company server holding your fill-ups, and nothing to sign up for. Read the privacy policy.

Install in a minute

It's not on the Play Store yet, so Android asks a couple of extra questions the first time.

  1. Download

    Tap Download the APK on your phone. The file is VehicleLog.apk.

  2. Allow the install

    Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or Files app for this once.

  3. Install anyway

    Play Protect may not recognise the app yet. Tap Install anyway. Later updates install from inside the app.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, no paid features. It started as a personal logbook for a car and a bike.

Does it work without internet?

Everything works offline, including your documents. Internet is only used for Drive backup, finding nearby pumps, road routes on trip maps, sign-in and update checks.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Turn on Google Drive backup, or save a backup file. On the new phone, sign in with Google and the app finds your backup; it shows what the backup holds before restoring anything.

Can I use it for more than one vehicle?

Yes, a car and a bike or the whole family's. Each has its own logbook, reminders, budget and theme colour; documents like your licence are shared.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It will be. Until then, releases are built and published straight from the source on GitHub, and the app updates itself.

Found a bug or have an idea?

In the app, go to More → Report a problem. It fills in your app and phone details for you.
